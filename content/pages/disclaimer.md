## Not immigration or legal advice

Visa, residency, employment-permit and citizenship articles describe rules as we understand them on the date stated. Immigration outcomes turn on individual facts, on the discretion of the officer handling your case, and on rules that the Ministry of Justice can and does change at short notice.

Nothing here is legal advice or a prediction of the outcome of any application. For authoritative answers, contact the **Korea Immigration Service Contact Centre on ☎ 1345** (multilingual) or a licensed Korean immigration attorney or administrative scrivener (*haengjeong-sasa*).

## Not tax advice

Tax articles are general descriptions. Your position depends on residency status, treaty relationships, income type, holding period, and facts we know nothing about. For authoritative answers contact the **National Tax Service English helpline on ☎ 126** or a licensed Korean tax accountant (*semusa*).

## Not medical advice

Where we cover healthcare, National Health Insurance or medical costs, we describe systems and prices — never treatment. Consult a qualified clinician for anything concerning your health.

## Translation
