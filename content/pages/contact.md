We read everything that arrives. We answer most of it within two business days (Seoul time).

## Email

**[editor@futureofkorea.com](mailto:editor@futureofkorea.com)** — one address, routed internally.

To help it reach the right person quickly, start your subject line with one of these tags:

| Tag | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| `CORRECTION` | A factual error in a published article. Include the URL and, if you can, the source that shows the correct figure. These jump the queue. |
| `TIP` | A story, a dataset, a filing, or a change nobody has written up yet. |
| `QUESTION` | Something you wanted an article to answer and it didn't. These directly shape what we publish next. |
| `ADVERTISING` | Sponsorship, direct display, or newsletter placement enquiries. |
| `LICENSING` | Republication, translation, or use of our charts and analysis. |
| `PRIVACY` | Data access, correction, or deletion requests under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or Korea's PIPA. |
| `LEGAL` | Copyright, takedown, or other legal notices. |

## Postal and business details

Future of Korea is an independent digital publication. For formal correspondence requiring a postal address, or for verification of our business registration details, email `LEGAL` and we will respond with the appropriate information.

## What we can't do

We can't give you personalised investment advice, tell you whether to buy a specific stock, review your visa application, or answer individual tax or immigration questions. We're a publication, not an adviser — see the [disclaimer](/disclaimer/). For visas, the Korea Immigration Service contact centre (☎ 1345) is the authoritative source; for tax, the National Tax Service English helpline (☎ 126) is.

## Pitching

We occasionally publish outside contributions from people with direct, verifiable expertise — practitioners, researchers, and people who have actually done the thing. We do not accept guest posts written to place a link, and we do not sell links in editorial. Pitches that lead with "I noticed your article and would love to contribute a guest post" are deleted unread.

## Response times

Corrections: same day where possible. Everything else: two business days. We're on Korea Standard Time (UTC+9), so if you write from North America you should expect a reply overnight.
